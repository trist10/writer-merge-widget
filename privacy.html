Privacy Policy

Writer Merge Tool
Effective date: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Writer Merge Tool (“the Topping”) handles information when installed and used with Zoho Bigin.

The Topping is provided by LPC Development (“Developer”, “we”, “us” or “our”).

1. Information processed by the Topping

The Topping may process information contained within your Zoho account as necessary to provide its document merge functionality. This may include:

  • Bigin record information referenced by a configured Zoho Writer merge template;
  • the identifier of the Bigin record being merged;
  • the configured Zoho Writer template identifier;
  • the Zoho API domain associated with the user's data centre;
  • generated PDF documents;
  • WorkDrive folder and file information required to save generated documents; and
  • WorkDrive file metadata, including filename, permalink and modified time, used to identify an existing merge.

The exact Bigin information included in a generated document depends on the fields and merge elements configured in your Zoho Writer template.

2. How information is used

Information is processed only as necessary to provide the Topping's functionality, including to:

  • generate a document from a Bigin record using Zoho Writer;
  • convert the generated document to PDF;
  • save the completed PDF into the user's Zoho WorkDrive account;
  • identify whether a PDF has already been generated for the same Bigin record by checking the Writer Merge Documents folder in WorkDrive; and
  • maintain the configuration required for the Topping to operate.

The current version of the Topping does not create custom Pipeline fields or write merge-tracking information such as a merge timestamp or generated-document URL to Bigin Pipeline records.

3. Where information is processed

The current version of the Topping uses Zoho services, including Zoho Bigin, Zoho Writer and Zoho WorkDrive.

The Topping does not intentionally transmit Bigin record content or generated PDFs to a server or database operated by the Developer as part of the normal document-generation process.

Generated PDFs are stored in the user's own Zoho WorkDrive environment.

Zoho's processing, storage, security, logging and data-centre practices are governed by Zoho's own terms and privacy policies. The Developer does not control Zoho's underlying infrastructure or data-centre arrangements.

4. Authentication and permissions

The Topping uses Zoho's authentication and connection mechanisms to access the Zoho services required for its operation.

The Topping does not ask users to provide their Zoho account password directly to the Developer.

Access is limited to the permissions authorised during installation or configuration of the Topping and required for its functionality.

5. Configuration information

The Topping stores limited configuration information within the user's Bigin organisation so that it can operate across sessions.

This configuration consists of:

  • the Zoho Writer template identifier selected by the user; and
  • the Zoho API domain corresponding to the user's Zoho data centre.

These values are used only to direct merge requests to the selected Writer template and the appropriate Zoho regional API endpoint.

6. Information provided through Zoho Marketplace

Zoho Marketplace may provide the Developer with certain information about users of the Topping, such as a user's name, email address or other account information, where permitted by Zoho Marketplace and the user's consent or installation choices.

If received, this information may be used for purposes such as:

  • providing technical support;
  • responding to enquiries;
  • administering the Marketplace listing;
  • communicating important information concerning the Topping; and
  • complying with legal or Marketplace obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

7. Support communications

If you contact the Developer for support, we may receive information that you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, screenshots, diagnostic information or descriptions of an issue.

You should avoid sending sensitive or unnecessary customer information when requesting support.

Support correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the issue, maintain support records or meet applicable legal requirements.

8. Data retention and deletion

Generated documents remain in your Zoho WorkDrive account until they are removed in accordance with your Zoho account settings and practices.

Configuration information stored within your Bigin organisation remains subject to your control within Zoho and to the lifecycle of the Topping and your Zoho organisation.

Removing or uninstalling the Topping does not necessarily delete documents or information previously created or stored within your Zoho account. Users should separately remove any files or retained information they no longer wish to keep.

Personal information received directly by the Developer through support or Marketplace interactions will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received or as required by law.

9. Disclosure of information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

Personal information held by the Developer may be disclosed only where reasonably necessary:

  • to provide support or operate the Topping;
  • with your consent;
  • to comply with applicable law, regulation or legal process; or
  • to protect legal rights or address fraud, security or misuse.

Zoho Bigin, Writer, WorkDrive and Marketplace are operated by Zoho and are subject to Zoho's own privacy and security practices.

Zoho is not responsible for the Developer's handling of personal information that is separately provided to or collected by the Developer.

10. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to minimise access to personal information and to limit processing to what is necessary for the operation and support of the Topping.

However, no electronic service or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security over their own Zoho accounts, users, permissions and devices.

11. Children's privacy

The Topping is intended for business use and is not directed toward children.

The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Topping.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning personal information held about you, including rights to request access, correction or deletion.

Most business data processed by the Topping remains within your own Zoho account and should ordinarily be managed directly through the applicable Zoho service.

For information held directly by the Developer, you may contact us using the details below.
